Stories Downloader

View the statuses you've already watched in your chat app or Business app, save photos and videos to your gallery, and share them. No storage permission: you pick the status folder once with Android's folder picker, and your files stay on your phone.

  • Photos, videos and saved statuses in separate tabs
  • Save or share several at once
  • Optional auto-save
  • Dark mode, 11 languages, phone and tablet layouts
